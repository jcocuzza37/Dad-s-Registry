The non-toxic guide

Know what the certifications actually mean.

Most dads have never heard of GOTS or OEKO-TEX. The guide explains each one in plain language.

GOTS

Global Organic Textile Standard — fabric grown AND processed without harmful chemicals. The highest bar for baby textiles.

OEKO-TEX

Tested against 350+ toxic substances. What matters is the finished product is safe — not just what it's made from.

GG Gold

GREENGUARD Gold — low chemical emissions from furniture, mattresses, and car seats. The standard for what baby breathes.

EWG ✓

EWG Verified — Environmental Working Group clean formula. What you want on detergent, cream, and cleaning products.
